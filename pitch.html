"This sounds really cool, but it sounds complex, isn't there a lot of risk baked into the system?"

Good question! We're absolutely managing a lot of risk! As a brokerage we have a lot of inherent leverage and counterparty risk that needs to be considered.

Risk is managed the same way it's managed in any venue with counterparty risk (below is not an exhaustive list):

  • Caps on cross-margining and asset deposits based on the risk of the custodian (CEX's, Chains, L2s, Issuers, etc. are all custodians)
  • Borrow and funding rates are utilized to manage liquidity and exposure skew
    • Note: t+ has to manage exposure skew more aggressively than say a perp exchange because our margin trades are filled by counter-side margin trades, spot trades, margin trades that are settled onchain (effectively spot trades)
  • Not Every Asset is enabled as collateral! Assets that are supported as collateral have:
    • Caps on both spot-margin and derivative based leverage based on asset liquidity
    • Maintenance and Initial margin haircuts based on price and liquidity volatility - IM haircuts move up as exposure skew increases
    • Cross-margin support is limited to majors
  • Robust oracle that aggregates volume and liquidity weighted prices from multiple on and off chain sources
  • Deposits are rebalanced between vaults to maintain withdrawal and settlement liquidity
  • Fills on long-tail assets support optional delayed margin checks to allow market makers to settle onchain before confirming the trade - if settlement fails, the trade reverts (like an RFQ)

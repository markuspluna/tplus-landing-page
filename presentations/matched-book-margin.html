TRUTH NUKE: Leverage isn't just for degenerate gambling
Capital efficiency improves liquidity.
Perps trade 3-5x spot volume, 10x+ during volatility.
Why perps are so capital efficient
PURELY SYNTHETIC
  • No underlying needed
  • Not constrained by lender supply
  • Just need a counterparty
LOW-RISK DELEVERAGING
  • Closing = unwinding a contract
  • ADL: force-close against counterparties
    • No market impact
    • Zero new liquidity needed
What perps get wrong
SCALING CREATES INSTABILITY
  • Flow imbalances → premiums → funding
  • Unpredictable execution costs
  • Unpredictable carry costs
NON-FUNGIBLE WITH SPOT
  • Every position is a contract
  • Cash settlement - delivery is impossible
  • Harms liquidity and UX
Matched Book Margin
HYBRID MARGIN ENGINE
1
NON-SYNTHETIC LEVERAGE

PRINCIPLE

OPTIMIZATION

Why doesn't everyone use MBM?
MBM requires composability
MBM needs COMPOSABLE SPOT
  • Settle imbalanced flow into AMMs
  • Liquidate skewed exposure (spot margin) externally
MBM needs COMPOSABLE DERIVATIVES
  • Cross-margined basis traders keep exposure and deposits balanced

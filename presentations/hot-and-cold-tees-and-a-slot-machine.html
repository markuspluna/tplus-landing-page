Recovery and Failover
LEADER GOES DOWN
  • View change → new leader elected
  • Surgical rollback — only unrecovered tail
  • Trading continues during failover
FULL NETWORK FAILURE
  • Council decrypts checkpoint key shards
  • L1 verifies: correct software + valid checkpoint
  • Best candidate promoted to leader
